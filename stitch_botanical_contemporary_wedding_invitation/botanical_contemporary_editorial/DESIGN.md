## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an evocative, tactile stationery experience translated into digital space for discerning couples and luxury ceremonies. Rooted in the visual culture of independent art publications like *Kinfolk* and *Cereal*, the identity draws upon sun-dappled Mediterranean architecture, raw limestone, fine-milled cotton paper, and wild botanical gardens. 

The aesthetic marries restrained minimalism with tactile organic warmth:
- Avoid sentimental tropes: no glitz, no lustrous metallic gradients, no cartoonish hearts, and no heavy-handed floral vector clichés.
- Emphasize deliberate silence: vast open plains of warm ecru canvas allow single moments of refined typography and photographic intimacy to hold weight.
- Foster calm anticipation: the interface feels like an heirloom paper suite arriving in a tactile envelope, grounded by deep olive pigment, raw linen neutrals, and deliberate, measured pacing.

## Colors

The palette takes inspiration from sun-dried raw fibers, limestone, pressed foliage, and vegetable-based letterpress ink:

- **Base Canvas & Warm Neutrals**: Canvas surfaces rely on Warm Cotton (`#FAF9F6`) and Natural Linen / Ecru (`#F4F1EA`, `#EAE5D9`). Never use stark digital `#FFFFFF` or cool synthetic greys.
- **Deep Olive (Primary)**: `#3F4739` and `#2C3326` serve as foundational anchors for key calls to action, formal borders, monogram elements, and solemn focal points.
- **Muted Sage Green (Secondary)**: `#8A9A86` and `#748570` denote soft interaction states, foliage tags, subtle badges, and secondary thematic accents.
- **Stone Accents (Tertiary)**: `#D9D4C7` and `#C7C0B0` provide hairline borders, ruled dividers, and tactile paper edge definitions.
- **Warm Deep Brown (Typography & Ink)**: `#221F1B` and `#2A2521` replace harsh digital black, mimicking rich mineral pigment pressed into heavyweight cotton stock.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical literary serif styling with razor-sharp editorial sans-serif metadata:

- **Serif Voice (EB Garamond)**: Carries romance, solemnity, and history. Applied to couple names, dates, quotes, ceremonial invitations, and architectural section leads. Italic cuts must be deployed selectively for emotional emphasis, botanical notes, or parental dedications.
- **Sans-Serif Utility (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Handles structured logistics, timelines, RSVP inputs, attire guidelines, and navigation tags.
- **Micro-Copy Tracking**: Sans-serif headers, timestamps, and badges must consistently apply expanded letter-spacing (`0.12em` to `0.18em`) and uppercase styling to evoke bespoke editorial captions.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout follows an asymmetrical, editorial publication structure with an intentional absence of aggressive structural enclosures:

- **Grid Strategy**: A 12-column dynamic fluid grid on desktop transitioning to a single-column stacked narrative on mobile. Breakpoints are set at `640px` (mobile), `1024px` (tablet/editorial split), and `1440px` (desktop canvas cap).
- **Whitespace Philosophy**: Negative space functions as a structural element. Sections require breathing margins of at least `space-xl` to mirror high-end print layouts.
- **Masonry & Asymmetry**: Event schedules, image galleries, and destination notes flow through organic staggered columns rather than stiff tabular formats. Off-center editorial anchors invite leisurely scrolling rather than hasty interaction.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve the authentic stationery spirit, traditional drop shadows, heavy blur filters, and synthetic depth models are strictly avoided:

- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is achieved purely through paper tones. A Warm Cotton (`#FAF9F6`) field placed atop a Natural Linen (`#F4F1EA`) viewport creates instant tactile layering without artifice.
- **Hairline Framing**: Structural separation uses ultra-subtle Stone borders (`#D9D4C7`, 1px solid) echoing deckle-edged paper sheets and fine blind debossing.
- **Ambient Paper Glow**: When an elevated surface is mandatory (such as an RSVP modal or floating dietary drawer), utilize a faint, widely diffused organic wash: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(34, 31, 27, 0.05)`. Avoid black or grey drop shadows.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural geometry. High-radius shapes and pill-shaped UI components are forbidden, as they introduce an overly casual, app-like demeanor:

- **Architectural Radii**: Container edges, input fields, image frames, and actionable modules employ an understated `0.25rem` radius, balancing the crispness of guillotine-trimmed paper with the natural warmth of cotton stock.
- **Botanical Cut-outs & Arches**: Decorative portrait frames and image highlights may utilize classical Romanesque single-arch tops (`rounded-t-[8rem]`), referencing Mediterranean stone courtyards and heritage cloister arcades.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary CTA**: Deep Olive (`#3F4739`) fill with Ecru (`#F4F1EA`) uppercase sans-serif text (`label-lg`), subtle 1px border. Hover transitions to `#2C3326` with smooth 300ms easing.
- **Secondary CTA**: Transparent fill, 1px hairline border in Deep Olive or Stone (`#D9D4C7`), text in `#221F1B`. Hover gently transitions background to `#FAF9F6`.
- **Text Action**: Editorial inline serif links featuring a persistent 1px underline positioned 4px below the baseline, colored in Muted Sage (`#748570`).

### Chips & Dress Code Swatches
- **Status & Event Chips**: Architectural rectangles with `0.25rem` corners, styled with a `#EAE5D9` background and `#3F4739` tracked uppercase typography (`label-sm`).
- **Fabric & Palette Swatches**: Circular or rectangular fabric chips presenting the wedding palette (linen, sage, terracotta, olive), accompanied by fabric type labels (e.g., *Raw Silk, Crisp Linen*) using `label-md`.

### Form Fields & RSVP
- **Text Inputs**: Flat, minimalist inputs with transparent backgrounds and an understated 1px bottom border (`#D9D4C7`). On focus, the line deepens to Deep Olive (`#3F4739`) without outer glows or browser rings.
- **Labels**: Positioned cleanly above each input in uppercase `label-sm`, set in `#748570`.

### Selection Controls
- **Radio & Checkboxes**: Custom square and circular markers with razor-thin 1px borders in `#3F4739`. Selected state displays an inner organic dot or fill in Deep Olive. Never use default OS controls.

### Cards & Editorial Modules
- **Itinerary & Venue Cards**: Avoid heavy borders and solid shadow boxes. Use seamless tonal shifts (e.g., `#FAF9F6` against `#F4F1EA`) with generous inner padding (`space-lg`), punctuated by hairline horizontal dividers.
- **Editorial Quotations & Milestones**: Centered serif typography (`headline-md`) framed with delicate vertical hairline rules in `#D9D4C7`.
