## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the regal dignity, scholastic achievement, and sartorial sophistication of a ceremonial graduation gala in Kinshasa. It captures the atmosphere of haute couture editorial print publications, merging literary intellectualism with the warmth of Central African ceremonial celebrations. 

The aesthetic is classical-editorial: marked by dramatic typographic hierarchy, deliberate pacing, ultra-refined hairline rules, and generous breathing room. Surfaces reject synthetic neons, aggressive drop-shadows, and tech-centric frosted glass. Instead, the interface adopts tactile, tactilely grounded print metaphors—recalling hand-pressed deckle-edge cotton rag paper, debossed academic folios, and bespoke archival invitations.

Interactions are measured, graceful, and unhurried. The emotional resonance must inspire quiet prestige, pride, reverence, and celebratory triumph for graduates and their distinguished guests.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in ceremonial warmth and organic gravitas:

- **Canvas Base (`#F5F0E8` / Warm Ivory):** The atmospheric bedrock of the application, evocative of heavy vintage archival stock. Replaces sterile digital white across full viewport backgrounds.
- **Primary Accent (`#6A2332` / Deep Wine Bordeaux):** The focal color of majesty, academic tenure, and distinction. Used for primary interactive triggers, focal crest emblems, key date demarcations, and active navigational accents.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C6A5A2` / Dusty Muted Rose):** Provides soft, romantic restraint. Applied to auxiliary tags, secondary badge outlines, hover fills, and floral/ceremonial callouts.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FAF7F2` / Off-White Parchment):** Used for elevated cards, modal sheets, and invitation inserts to lift content off the warm ivory canvas with delicate contrast.
- **Typography & Structural Rules (`#332B29` / Deep Ink Brown):** The foundational text color. Prevents the clinical starkness of pure `#000000` while preserving effortless legibility and literary heritage.
- **Hairline Border Rule (`#E3DACD` / Muted Ochre-Bone):** Used for 0.5px and 1px structural dividing lines and ornamental frames.

## Typography

Typography drives the editorial layout. **EB Garamond** functions as the primary literary voice, lending classical balance, sculpted serifs, and historic gravitas to ceremony dates, laureate names, and formal salutations. Pair italic styles of EB Garamond with standard weights to create high-fashion editorial variety in pull quotes and honorary dedications.

**Manrope** supplies a quiet, neutral, geometric balance. Its role is strictly functional: rendering metadata, RSVP inputs, programmatic schedules, addresses, and logistical instructions without competing with the serifs. All labels and metadata badges are set in uppercase Manrope with deliberate letter-spacing (`0.12em` to `0.16em`) to emulate luxury gallery and couture catalog notation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system that prioritizes asymmetrical editorial spreads, deliberate margins, and generous vertical intervals:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `3rem` (48px) gutters and `4.5rem` (72px) outer margins. Key sections use deliberate whitespace offsets (e.g., spanning 8 centered columns or a 7/5 editorial asymmetrical split).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `2rem` (32px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) canvas margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) canvas margins.

Vertical rhythm is intentionally expansive: invitation panels and ceremony segments must never feel dense. Section separations rely on open spacing (`space-xl`) combined with delicate centered editorial rules rather than thick divider bands.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately excludes high-gloss digital elevation, ambient drop shadows, multi-tiered floating surfaces, and blur filters. Depth is established exclusively through print-inspired physical techniques:

- **Layered Paper Tone:** Depth is achieved by placing `#FAF7F2` (Off-White Parchment) content blocks against the `#F5F0E8` (Warm Ivory) base, delineating boundaries purely through value contrast.
- **Hairline Framing:** Elevation is bounded by single-pixel rules (`#E3DACD`). For featured laureate folios and RSVP modal containers, double hairline borders (an outer 1px border separated by 4px from an inner 0.5px hairline) evoke vintage embossed stationery.
- **Debossed Anchors:** Select recessed interactive surfaces (such as text input fields and dropdown wells) use a crisp interior line or a 1px solid rule underneath, mimicking debossed card indents.

## Shapes

The shape architecture relies entirely on **Sharp (`0`)** corners. Corners remain strictly orthogonal (0px radius) across all interactive cards, containers, buttons, dialogs, and image viewports. 

This strict geometric discipline reflects archival paper trimming, traditional stationery stocks, and formal diplomas. The crisp rectilinear forms frame imagery and typography with editorial rigor.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (RSVP / Confirm Attendance):** Sharp rectangular footprint. Solid `#6A2332` (Wine Bordeaux) background with `#FAF7F2` text set in uppercase `label-lg` with `0.14em` tracking. Zero border-radius. Padding: `16px 36px`. Hover transitions gently to `#4D1823`.
- **Secondary Action (View Programme / Add to Calendar):** Transparent background with a 1px hairline border of `#6A2332`. Text in `#6A2332`. Hover fills with `#FAF7F2` and deepens the border to `#332B29`.
- **Text Link:** EB Garamond Italic (`body-lg`) with an unbroken 1px underline positioned 4px below the baseline, colored in `#C6A5A2`.

### Cards & Invitation Sleeves
- Background: `#FAF7F2`. Sharp edges.
- Outlines: 1px continuous solid border in `#E3DACD`. 
- Padding: Generous interior spacing (`space-lg` desktop, `space-md` mobile). 
- Inner Accents: Optional nested inset frame (1px border placed 6px inside the outer perimeter) used exclusively for the prime graduation convocational summons.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Text fields do not use enclosed four-sided floating boxes. Instead, they feature an open editorial baseline: transparent background with a single bottom hairline rule (1px `#C6A5A2`).
- Focus state: Bottom border sharpens to 1.5px `#6A2332`.
- Field labels: Upper-case `label-md` tracking above the line in `#332B29`, accompanied by input text in `EB Garamond` 18px.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square glyphs (16px × 16px) with sharp 0px corners and a 1px border in `#332B29`.
- Checked state: Filled with `#6A2332` bearing an interior sharp diamond or ivory square inset. No rounded organic checks.

### Chips & Badges
- Slender, rectilinear tags. 1px hairline perimeter in `#C6A5A2` over `#FAF7F2` background. 
- Typography: Uppercase `label-sm` in `#6A2332`. Internal padding: `4px 10px`.

### Ceremony Timeline / Itinerary List
- Vertical hairline rule in `#E3DACD` down the left axis.
- Nodes are indicated by tiny 4px × 4px solid `#6A2332` squares positioned precisely along the line.
- Time stamps set in `label-lg` uppercase; event titles set in `headline-sm` EB Garamond.

### Editorial Dividers & Monograms
- Hairline divider: 1px width in `#E3DACD`, spanning full width or shortened to 120px centered with a small centered lozenge (◆) ornament in `#C6A5A2`.
