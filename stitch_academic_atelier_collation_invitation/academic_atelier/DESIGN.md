## Brand & Style

This design system draws its foundation from classical academic dignity fused with the precision of Swiss modernism and contemporary tropical brutalist architecture found in Central African institutions. Created for an elite university collation in Kinshasa, it rejects celebratory clichés—confetti, artificial glossy gradients, and generic corporate SaaS cards—in favor of a sovereign, monumental editorial presence.

The aesthetic philosophy balances two primary tensions:
1. **Architectural Discipline:** Grounded by a visible structural armature, ultra-fine 1px hairline rules, structured tabular data, uppercase monospaced coordinate markers, and oversized indexing numbers (e.g., `01`, `02`).
2. **Editorial Gravitas:** Evoked through deliberate typographical contrast, pairing an authoritative, high-contrast serif display with hyper-disciplined, wide-tracked grotesque titling and body text.

The interface serves as an exhibition space, a public archive, and an accreditation platform. It treats graduate registries, faculty ceremonial programs, and admission credentials with the tactile reverence of an art biennial catalogue or a state ceremonial gazette. The emotional response is solemn, prestigious, intellectual, and uncompromisingly modern.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from mineral textures, academic regalia, and archival paper stock. The baseline experience operates strictly in an architectural light canvas:

- **Craie Chaude (`#F4F1EA` - Neutral Ground):** The fundamental physical canvas. Not clinical digital white, but an absorbent, tactile plaster surface. All page containers, modal surfaces, and broad plains originate here.
- **Rouge Vin (`#813E47` - Primary Accent):** The ceremonial dye. Used for focal academic authorities, critical status indicators, primary actions, and solemn focal lines. Never used as a decorative flood; applied with clinical intent.
- **Bleu Poussiéreux (`#637A87` - Secondary Mineral):** Atmospheric cool stone. Deployed for structural sub-headers, tertiary button states, architectural line accents, and badge backgrounds.
- **Bleu Encre Doux (`#35464D` - Tertiary Ink):** Deep archival pigment. Acts as the principal contrast vehicle against the Craie Chaude background. All structural typography, primary outlines, and monospaced indices use this color or its derived opacities.
- **Gris Pierre (`#AAA49B` - Neutral Rule):** The divider tone. Used for 1px hairlines, subtle coordinate grids, inactive pagination markers, and unselected structural boundaries.

### Architectural Tints & Functional Applications
- **Surface Canvas:** `#F4F1EA`
- **Surface Elevated (Parchment Card):** `#FFFFFF` with an immediate bounding hairline of `#AAA49B` (at 40% opacity).
- **Ink Primary:** `#35464D` (High contrast text, titles, prominent metadata).
- **Ink Muted:** `#637A87` (Captions, timestamps, secondary indices).
- **Divider Hairline:** `#AAA49B` (Rendered strictly at 1px solid without drop shadows).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a dialogue between historical academic authority and rationalist technical precision.

- **Playfair Display (Serif):** Reserved for academic discourse, titles of conferral, keynote statements, and graduate honoree names. Use the italic cut sparingly for Latin honors (*Magna Cum Laude*), addresses of jurisdiction (e.g., *Kinshasa, RDC*), and ceremonial dates.
- **Inter (Grotesque Body):** Engineered for sustained legibility within long-form manifestos, speech transcripts, and institutional policies. Line heights remain generous (`1.6` to `1.7`) to maintain an airy, printed book atmosphere.
- **Space Grotesk (Constructivist Metadata):** Always deployed in upper-case for tags, accreditation codes, seating coordinates, and structural markers. The wide tracking (`0.14em` to `0.2em`) functions as a technical counter-weight to the organic luxury of the serif display.
- **Oversized Indexing Numbers:** The `index-num` token represents structural monoliths (`01`, `02`, `03`) flanking content sections, anchored to grid coordinates to break up white plains.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on an unyielding 12-column Swiss modular grid on desktop (1440px max content column) and a 4-column structured grid on mobile devices.

### Grid Anatomy & Layout Rhythms
- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12 columns with a fixed `2rem` (32px) gutter and a broad `4rem` (64px) outer margin. This margin creates a defensive perimeter of blank space, reminiscent of broadsheet print borders.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2.5rem` canvas margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` outer canvas margin.

### Architectural Division Mechanics
Sections do not blend via background color shifts. Instead, the layout uses explicit perimeter lines:
- Primary horizontal partitions are bounded by single 1px solid hairlines (`#AAA49B` at 60% opacity).
- Asymmetrical column offsets are mandatory: index numerals occupy 2 columns, section narrative spans 6 columns, and metadata/micro-labels anchor to the trailing 4 columns.
- Vertical writing modes (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`) are utilized for edge coordinates, date-stamps, and classification markers along the page borders.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids digital drop shadows, multi-layered diffuse blurs, and neomorphic extrusions. Depth is conveyed strictly through **tonal stratification and physical framing**:

1. **Zero Shadows:** Elevation tokens with `box-shadow` values are forbidden. All elements sit directly on the plane.
2. **Hairline Framing:** Interfaces simulate high-grade paper laid upon an architectural cutting mat. Cards and modals achieve separation via a 1px solid perimeter boundary (`#35464D` or `#AAA49B`) coupled with an internal background shift from Craie Chaude (`#F4F1EA`) to pure Blanc Calque (`#FFFFFF`).
3. **Layered Translucency (Tracing Paper/Calque):** For sticky navigation bars and modal overlays, use a crisp, frosted architectural veil: `background-color: rgba(244, 241, 234, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`. Hairline base borders remain 100% opaque.
4. **Physical Insets:** Recessed elements (such as signature lines, barcode areas, and verification cutouts) rely on precise 1px inset rules rather than dark inner shadows.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**. Every corner in this system forms a precise 90-degree right angle (0px border-radius).

- **Geometric Justification:** Soft or rounded corners evoke casual consumer software. A collation of this caliber demands the architectural severity of cut paper, institutional ledgers, structural steel, and monolith stone plaques.
- **Exceptions:** No element—whether buttons, accreditation passes, avatar portraits, input boxes, or tags—may utilize rounded corners. All items are precision-cut rectangles.
- **Perforations & Notches:** For physical accreditation cards and ticket units, angled 45-degree chamfers or precise 12px circular punch-outs (simulating mechanical lanyard clips or ticket stubs) may be cut into the bounding edges via CSS clip-paths, maintaining the mechanical print heritage.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Sovereign:** Background `#813E47`, text `#F4F1EA`. Font: `Space Grotesk`, uppercase, `12px`, letter spacing `0.18em`. Zero border-radius. Padding: `16px 32px`. Hover state: background transitions instantly to `#35464D`.
- **Secondary Monastic:** Transparent background, 1px solid boundary `#35464D`, text `#35464D`. Hover state: Inverted to background `#35464D`, text `#F4F1EA`.
- **Text Link Architectural:** Text `#35464D`, underlined with a 1px solid rule offset by 6px. On hover, the rule color shifts to `#813E47`.

### The Accreditation Pass (Bespoke Pass Component)
- **Structure:** A vertically oriented proportion (3:5 ratio) echoing an international pavilion badge or biennial VIP pass.
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF` surrounded by a 1px solid `#35464D` border.
- **Header:** Features an oversized monospaced category code (`ACC-2025 // DRC`) sitting above a bold hairline separator.
- **Core Area:** Features high-contrast photographic portraiture (rendered in black and white or high-key sepia), candidate matriculation number, faculty designation in uppercase `Space Grotesk`, and graduate name in `Playfair Display`.
- **Footer:** Machine-readable barcode or high-density cryptographic QR matrix, with a small mechanical punched notch at the top center edge for a lanyard fixture.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Fields:** Transparent ground, strictly bordered on the bottom with a 1px solid `#35464D` rule (no boxed inputs). Labels float above in `label-caps` (`Space Grotesk`, uppercase, tracked `0.2em`). Placeholder text rendered in `#AAA49B`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square boxes (`16px × 16px`) with a 1px solid `#35464D` border. Checked states feature a solid `#813E47` fill leaving a 2px interior gutter, or a single raw cross (`×`) for check marks.

### Cards & Editorial Rosters
- **Faculty & Graduate Cards:** Un-elevated boxes bound by a hairline border (`1px solid #AAA49B`). Imagery must be full-bleed or set with a uniform 16px white paper matting.
- **Hover Behavior:** No Y-axis floating or elevation lift. On hover, the 1px perimeter transitions from `#AAA49B` to `#813E47`, and the index number changes to italic.

### Lists & Timetables
- **Program Schedules:** Arranged as an architectural table. Columns demarcated by 1px vertical hairlines. Timestamps rendered in `Space Grotesk`, session titles in `Playfair Display`, and discussants/speakers in `Inter`. Alternate rows do not use zebra striping; structural separation is managed exclusively through border hairlines and generous vertical cell padding (`24px`).
